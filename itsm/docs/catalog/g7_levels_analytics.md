# Service level management (SLA/OLA/UC/XLA), workforce and agent experience, analytics/reporting/KPIs, security operations/risk/compliance

Consolidated, deduplicated catalog for module keys `slm`, `workforce`, `analytics`, `security` (research snapshot 2026-11-07). Entry format: **ID Name** · Persian name · tier · AI flag — what it does and how. *Src* = tools/frameworks that have it; *Impl* = hint for our metadata-driven record engine.
Tool tags: SN ServiceNow, JSM Jira Service Management/Atlassian, FS Freshservice, BMC BMC Helix, ME ManageEngine SDP, Halo, Xurrent, TOPdesk, Ivanti, InvGate, SysAid, SMAX, ZD Zendesk, GLPI, iTop. Tier: P0 = enterprise v1 must-have, P1 = differentiator to build, P2 = later. Shared conventions: every record carries the access columns of the access model (`tenant_id, domain_id, workspace_id, security_level_id, restricted_group_id, classification, assignment_group_id, assignee_id, company_id, org_unit_id, location_id, service_id, acl_keys`); child rows (SLA instances, scores, evaluations) copy the parent's `acl_keys`. AI always runs as the calling user (retrieval and queries filtered before the model) and every AI artefact is logged (`ai_run`).

## Module: slm — مدیریت سطح خدمت (SLA/OLA/UC/XLA)

### Features
- **SLM-001 Agreement register** · ثبت توافق‌نامه‌ها · P0 · AI:no — One record per agreement: kind SLA (customer), OLA (internal team), UC (supplier, linked to a contract), SIAM collaboration agreement or FLSA; owner, version, validity, review date, compliance and at-risk targets, linked services/offerings/CIs/customers, provider group, nested child agreements. *Src:* ITIL 4/5; ISO 20000-1 §8.3.3–8.3.4; FitSM PR2; COBIT APO09.03; SIAM BoK; SN; BMC; ME; FS. *Impl:* `sla_agreement`; scope by domain/workspace/company; documents in the platform document register.
- **SLM-002 Agreement lifecycle & renewal** · چرخه عمر و تمدید توافق‌نامه · P0 · AI:no — Eight-stage lifecycle: requirements → viability (technical/financial) → draft → negotiation → communication/enablement → active with periodic review → renewal (prolongation) → withdrawal; approval of each version; renewal and review reminders; expired agreements stop attaching new SLAs. *Src:* ITIL v5 SLM; ISO 20000-1 §8.3.3; COBIT APO09.05. *Impl:* state machine + approvals + planned-interval scheduler (RRULE review cadence).
- **SLM-003 Agreement structures, tiers & templates** · ساختار چندسطحی و الگوهای سطح خدمت · P1 · AI:no — Service-based, customer-based and multi-level structures; Gold/Silver/Bronze packages vs negotiated targets; an SLA template per catalogue offering (offer = service + size/region + SLA template). *Src:* ITIL SLM PG; IT4IT Service Offer; SN service commitments. *Impl:* `sla_template` cloned into agreements; offering → default agreement link.
- **SLM-004 Catalogue linkage & outcome targets** · اتصال به کاتالوگ و اهداف پیامدمحور · P0 · AI:no — Validation that every SLA references a catalogue service/offering, and that outcome/experience measures sit beside operational targets ("watermelon" prevention); metric category tag (utility, warranty, experience, sustainability). *Src:* ITIL 4 Foundation; ITIL v5. *Impl:* save-time rule on `sla_agreement`; `metric_category` on targets.
- **SLM-005 Service level target definitions** · تعریف اهداف سطح خدمت · P0 · AI:no — Target (service target) = metric (first response, response, resolution, fulfilment, time-to-approve, close-after-resolve, availability %, throughput, error rate, experience score, custom), goal, measurement window, warning thresholds, impact cost per unit of missed time, display label. Targets attach per priority, catalogue item or CI tier. *Src:* BMC SVT; SN `contract_sla`; JSM SLA metrics; FS; ME; ISO 20000-1 §8.3.3. *Impl:* `sla_definition` (metadata; applies to any table) + `sla_goal`.
- **SLM-006 Availability & outage targets** · اهداف دسترس‌پذیری و قطعی · P0 · AI:no — Availability % = time available ÷ (agreed time − excluded time), from outage records per service/offering or CI outage records against the service schedule; planned maintenance excluded only if the agreement says so. *Src:* BMC Outage-Availability/CI Outage; SN service commitments; ITIL. *Impl:* consumes `outage` records (continuity module) per review period; business-time service for agreed hours.
- **SLM-007 Performance-monitoring & compliance-only targets** · اهداف پایش کارایی و انطباق صرف · P2 · AI:no — KPI thresholds over infrastructure metrics (CPU, app response) evaluated by a collector (Met/Missed/Warning/Unknown), and compliance-only targets whose data was computed externally and is evaluated per review period. *Src:* BMC goal types. *Impl:* metric feed from event module or `metric` layer; no per-record instances.
- **SLM-008 Ordered applicability policies** · سیاست‌های اعمال SLA با ترتیب اولین‌تطابق · P0 · AI:no — Conditions on any record field and on requester/customer attributes (VIP, department, location, customer tier), match ALL/ANY, ordered top-to-bottom with first match wins and a catch-all; disabled (not deleted) when used. *Src:* JSM goals (JQL, customerDetail/organizationDetail); FS `applicable_to`/`evaluate_on`; ME Organize; SN conditions. *Impl:* condition AST of the record engine; validate overlaps on save; cap goals (JSM: 90 per SLA).
- **SLM-009 Priority-grouped goals** · اهداف گروه‌بندی‌شده بر اساس اولویت · P0 · AI:no — Inside one goal, per-priority target and calendar with an "all remaining priorities" bucket; on priority change the elapsed time carries over to the new target; per-target business vs calendar hours; ME "resolve irrespective of operational hours" for critical work. *Src:* JSM; FS; ME; SN. *Impl:* `sla_goal.priority_targets` jsonb; goal re-evaluation on field change writes `goal_change` event.
- **SLM-010 Clock conditions** · شرایط شروع، توقف موقت، پایان و بازنشانی ساعت · P0 · AI:no — Start, pause (with when-to-resume), stop, cancel (when-to-cancel) and reset conditions; events such as created, entered state, assignee from/to unassigned, comment by customer, resolution set/cleared, due date set; pause by state category (waiting for customer/vendor) via a "timer off" flag on states; pluggable condition classes. *Src:* SN; JSM; BMC Start/Stop/Exclude When; FS; ME. *Impl:* event-sourced state machine per (record, definition); deterministic evaluation order on every write.
- **SLM-011 Retroactive start & pause** · شروع و توقف عطف‌به‌ماسبق · P1 · AI:no — Start time set from a record date field (e.g. created) when the SLA attaches later; optionally replay pauses that happened before attach. *Src:* SN. *Impl:* replay `record_history` intervals for the instance.
- **SLM-012 Relative durations** · مدت نسبی (پایان روز کاری بعد و …) · P1 · AI:no — Targets like "end of next business day" or "breach on due date" computed by a named function against the record, task or SLA schedule. *Src:* SN relative duration. *Impl:* registry of server functions in the business-time service.
- **SLM-013 Business calendars & schedule sources** · تقویم‌های کاری و منبع زمان‌بندی · P0 · AI:no — Named calendars with IANA time zone, working days, several slots per day (lunch break), holidays, special hours and child schedules per site/group/region/service/provider; 24×7 calendar; copy calendars; schedule source (definition, CI, task field) and time-zone source (caller, caller location, CI location, task location, definition); viewer sees times in own zone. *Src:* SN cmn_schedule; JSM calendars; FS business hours; ME operational hours; BMC business entity. *Impl:* shared `business_calendar`; precomputed working-minute index per zone/day for O(1) interval sums.
- **SLM-014 Jalali calendars & Iranian holiday feed** · تقویم جلالی و تعطیلات رسمی ایران · P0 · AI:yes — Jalali-native calendars (week Saturday–Friday, Thursday half-day option), official solar holidays plus lunar holidays that shift every year, provincial holidays and ad-hoc government closures (pollution, heat, elections). Holiday sets are versioned per year; AI reads a pasted closure announcement and proposes entries for human approval. *Src:* new (gap in all tools); analytics_ux Jalali notes. *Impl:* `holiday_set`/`holiday(kind, province, source, approved_by)`; open clocks recomputed and logged when a set changes.
- **SLM-015 SLA instances with dual clocks** · نمونه‌های SLA با ساعت دیواری و کاری · P0 · AI:no — One instance per record × definition: stage (in progress, paused, completed, cancelled) with warning and has-breached flags, start, planned breach time, original breach time, stop time, elapsed/remaining/% in wall-clock and business time, pause duration, breach reason code. *Src:* SN `task_sla`; BMC measurement states; JSM cycles; ITIL. *Impl:* `sla_instance` (copies parent `acl_keys`) + append-only `sla_event`; breach_at stored for indexed sorting.
- **SLM-016 Multi-cycle SLAs** · SLAهای چندچرخه‌ای · P1 · AI:no — A start event after a stop opens a new cycle (reopened record, each customer comment restarting "time to respond"); completed cycles are immutable; filters can ask "last cycle" or "ever breached". *Src:* JSM. *Impl:* `sla_instance.cycle_no`; `everBreached()` = exists cycle with breach.

### Data model
State categories: new / active / waiting / resolved / closed / canceled.
- `sla_agreement` **AGR-** — kind {sla, ola, uc, collaboration, flsa}, name, version, owner_id, provider_group_id, customer company_id/org_unit_id, supplier_id, contract_id, service_ids[], offering_ids[], ci_ids[], parent_agreement_id, valid_from/to, review_rrule, next_review_at, compliance_target, at_risk_target, review_period, workload_limits jsonb, exclusions jsonb, credit_rule_id, document_id, xla_clause. States: draft, requirements, viability (new) → negotiation, awaiting_approval (waiting) → active, under_review (active) → withdrawn, expired (closed); rejected (canceled).
- `sla_template` — package (gold/silver/bronze) cloned into agreements.

### KPIs
- SLA attainment % = instances met ÷ (met + breached), completed in period (per definition, service, customer, priority, group); response and resolution attainment separately.
- Breach count and trend; at-risk count = active instances with pct ≥ warning or P(breach) ≥ 0.7.
- Paused-time share = Σ business pause ÷ Σ (business elapsed + pause).
- OLA attainment; UC attainment; OLA/UC contribution = SLA breaches whose record also had a breached OLA/UC ÷ SLA breaches.
- Agreement compliance % per review period vs compliance/at-risk target.
- Availability % = (agreed time − downtime) ÷ (agreed time − excluded time) × 100.
- Service-credit exposure = Σ credits calculated; credits paid/received.
- Catalogue coverage = live customer-facing services with an active SLA ÷ live customer-facing services; % SLAs with measurable targets.
- % agreements overdue for review = active with next_review_at < today ÷ active; % service reviews held on schedule; % review actions closed by due date.
- CSAT % = responses scoring 4–5 ÷ responses × 100 (typical 85–95%); response rate = responses ÷ invitations.
- NPS = % promoters (9–10) − % detractors (0–6); CES = mean 1–7 (target ≥ 5.5).
- Happiness = (count 9–10 − count 0–6) ÷ count × 100; median perceived lost time per ticket.
- XLA score mean and band distribution; % records in "Poor" band.
- Watermelon gap = SLA attainment % − experience % (Happiness normalised as (H + 100) ÷ 2).
- SLO attainment = SLI ≥ SLO over window; error budget remaining = 1 − (1 − SLI) ÷ (1 − SLO); burn rate = (1 − SLI_w) ÷ (1 − SLO).
- Complaints per 1,000 closed records; complaint closure time.
- Prediction quality: Brier score; precision/recall at threshold; median warning lead time before breach.

### KPIs
- Resolved per agent per day; AHT = (handle + wrap-up time) ÷ handled items; backlog per agent = open assigned ÷ active agents.
- Utilisation = handle time ÷ paid time (typical 50–70%); occupancy = handling time ÷ logged-in time (chat/phone 75–85%); schedule adherence = time in scheduled state ÷ scheduled time (≥ 90–95%); shrinkage = (paid − available) ÷ paid (≈ 30%).
- Capacity utilisation = Σ teams (items ÷ capacity) × 100; mean time to pickup (idle) per group.
- Forecast MAPE = mean(|actual − forecast| ÷ actual); bias = Σ(forecast − actual) ÷ Σ actual; required FTE = volume × AHT ÷ (productive hours × occupancy).
- Reassignment rate, reopen rate, CSAT and SLA attainment per agent/team.
- QA score = Σ(weight × score) ÷ max × 100; QA coverage = evaluated ÷ handled; calibration κ; AI-assist acceptance = suggestions used ÷ shown.
- Skills coverage = critical services with ≥ N qualified people ÷ critical services; single-point-of-knowledge count; certification rate; training hours per FTE; time to competency.
- eNPS = % promoters − % detractors; attrition = leavers ÷ average headcount.
- On-call: pages per shift (≤ 2 per 12 h), night pages per person, % time on call (≤ 25%), fairness = max ÷ median pages per person; toil % = toil hours ÷ engineering hours (≤ 50%).

- **ANA-001 Governed metric catalog (semantic layer)** · کاتالوگ شاخص‌های حاکمیت‌شده · P0 · AI:no — Each metric defined once: formula over facts, filters, measure, unit, direction, clock (calendar or business, pauses subtracted), grain, owner, certification badge, data source, refresh, version; dimensions and glossary; reused by lists, reports, dashboards, SLAs and AI answers so numbers match everywhere; median/p90 shown beside means. *Src:* SN indicators; JSM Insights catalog/glossary; BMC/ME semantic layer; ITIL M&R; ISO 20000-1 §9.1; COBIT MEA01. *Impl:* `metric` versioned metadata; compiler to SQL.
- **ANA-002 KPI content packs** · بسته‌های آماده شاخص · P0 · AI:no — Prebuilt indicators and dashboards per practice (incident, problem, change, request, knowledge, SLA, CMDB health, change success, security, workforce) plus DORA, SRE, KCS, XLA, Kanban/Flow and HDI packs; each module's KPIs registered here. *Src:* SN content packs; standards metric packs; ITIL practice metrics. *Impl:* packs = metric + dashboard JSON.
- **ANA-003 Scheduled snapshots & drill-back** · عکس‌برداری زمان‌بندی‌شده از شاخص‌ها · P0 · AI:no — Automated, manual, formula and external indicators collected daily/weekly/monthly at one point in time per source, historical backfill, contributing record ids kept so "which records made last month's backlog" is answerable; late events recomputed by watermark. *Src:* SN Performance Analytics. *Impl:* `indicator_score(metric, version, period, breakdown, element, value, record_ids_ref, collected_at)`.
- **ANA-004 Breakdowns & buckets** · ابعاد تفکیک و دسته‌های بازه‌ای · P1 · AI:no — Dimensions (priority, group, category, service, location…) with field or scripted mappings, bucket groups (age ranges), two levels, top-N elements plus "other". *Src:* SN breakdowns. *Impl:* `dimension`, `bucket_group`.
- **ANA-005 Targets, thresholds & KPI alerts** · هدف، آستانه و هشدار شاخص · P0 · AI:no — Target and red/amber/green thresholds per scope, threshold events to notifications and subscriptions, baseline and history. *Src:* SN; ITIL goal cascade; ISO 20000-1 §9.1. *Impl:* `target_threshold` rules evaluated after each collection.

### Data model
- `metric` **MET-** (key, name_fa/en, formula, measure, filters, unit, direction, clock, grain, owner_id, certified, version, valid_from), `dimension`, `bucket_group`, `indicator_score`, `target_threshold`, `score_comment`.
- `report` **RPT-** and `dashboard` **DSH-** (definition JSON, owner, folder, version; draft → published → archived; trashed), `artifact_share` (principal, right {view, edit, manage}), `delivery_schedule` **SCD-** (rrule, tz, channel, recipients, format, skip_if_empty, run_as_owner) + `delivery_log`, `published_snapshot` **SNP-**.
- `insight` **INS-** (metric, type, effect, contributors, narrative; new → seen → actioned/dismissed), `ai_query_log`, `process_mining_run` **PMR-**.
- `cir_item` **IMP-** (idea, submitted (new) → assessed, approved (waiting) → planned, in_progress, implemented, benefits_measured (active/resolved) → closed; rejected, deferred, archived (canceled)), `nonconformity` **NC-**, `management_review` **MRV-**, `report_spec`, `assessment` **ASM-** (framework, scope, items, evidence, current, target), `objective`/`key_result`, `dq_rule`/`dq_result`.
- Facts listed in ANA-018 with date (Jalali + Gregorian), user, group, service, CI, category, location dimensions.
Access scoping: reports/dashboards are shared definitions; data is always run-as-viewer (`report_on`, `report_aggregate`, `publish_snapshot` rights); CIR items, nonconformities and assessments use record scopes (`own`, `group`, `unit_subtree`, `service`); NL queries logged with `on_behalf_of`; small-cell suppression k (default 5).

### KPIs
- % KPIs with owner and target = owned-and-targeted KPIs ÷ catalogued KPIs; report automation rate = auto-generated ÷ all reports; report timeliness = delivered by due ÷ due.
- Data-to-errors ratio; field completeness % per key field; % reports without errors; stakeholder satisfaction with reports.
- Dashboard adoption = viewers active in 30 days ÷ licensed users; NL answer acceptance = pinned or kept ÷ answers; insight action rate = actioned ÷ shown.
- CIR: % items with measured benefits; median idea → implemented time; % improvements meeting expected value; ROI = (benefit − cost) ÷ cost; active improvement owners.
- % management reviews on schedule; % corrective actions effective; NC closure on time.
- Flow efficiency = value-adding time ÷ lead time; lead/cycle time p50/p85; throughput; WIP; work-item age; rolled %C&A = Π %C&A per step.
- Maturity gap = Σ importance × (target − current) per practice.
- AI value: Δ MTTR (assisted − unassisted); AI resolution rate; cost per AI resolution; hours saved.
- DORA pack: change lead time, deployment frequency, failed deployment recovery time, change fail rate, deployment rework rate.

### Beyond the market
- RTL-native dashboards with Jalali time intelligence, Persian digits and mirrored charts; leaders offer RTL only partially (BMC added Arabic in 26.3, Jira Cloud has none).
- Persian natural-language analytics ("تیکت‌های بحرانی سه ماه اول ۱۴۰۵") over one governed metric layer, so AI answers equal dashboard numbers, under the viewer's row-level security.
- Reports and alerts delivered to Bale, Eitaa or Telegram and SMS summaries; Persian PDF/Excel with correct shaping and Jalali dates.
- Narratives, insights and forecasts run on the tenant's local model on-premises; nothing leaves the network.
- Insight engine aware of Iranian calendar effects (Nowruz dips, end-of-year peaks) so they are annotated, not flagged as anomalies.
- Offline Windows dashboards from scoped snapshots.

### Data model
- `security_incident` **SIR-** (secure workspace; category, severity, impact, risk_score, first_malicious_at, detected_at, contained_at, recovered_at, personal_data_breach, lead_id; draft (new) → analysis, contain, eradicate, recover (active) → review (resolved) → closed; canceled), `security_task` **SIT-**, `observable` (type, normalized value, verdict, enrichment, ttl) + `incident_observable`, `evidence_item` (hash, custody log), `regulatory_clock` **REG-** (regime, trigger_at, deadlines, submissions).
- `vulnerability` (CVE, CVSS 4.0 vector, EPSS, KEV, due date, advisories, solutions), `vulnerable_item` **VIT-** (ci_id, vulnerability_id, source, first_detected_at, last_seen_at, risk_score, rating, due_at, VEX status; open (new) → in_remediation (active) → deferred/exception (waiting) → fixed/verified (resolved) → closed; false_positive (canceled)), `vulnerability_group` **VG-**, `vuln_exception` **EXC-**, `compliance_result`.
- `control` **CTL-** (27002 attributes, owner, frequency), `control_requirement`, `grc_entity` **ENT-**, `control_instance`, `control_test` **TST-**, `control_indicator`, `soa_entry`, `csf_profile` **PRF-**.
- `policy` **POL-** (draft → review → published → retired), `attestation_campaign` **ATT-**, `policy_exception` **WVR-**, `obligation` **OBL-**, `regulatory_alert` **RAL-**, `grc_issue` **ISS-**.
- `risk` **RSK-** (identified (new) → assessed, treatment_planned (active) → treating, monitoring (active) → accepted (waiting, with expiry) → closed; retired (canceled)), `risk_assessment` **RA-**, `risk_treatment`, `risk_acceptance`, `kri` + readings, `risk_event` **LOSS-**, `risk_scale` (versioned), `risk_appetite`.
- `audit_engagement` **AUD-**, `audit_plan`, `workpaper`, `audit_finding` **FND-**, `evidence_request`; `processing_activity` **ROPA-**, `dpia` **DPIA-**, `data_subject_request` **DSR-**; `dlp_policy`, `dlp_event`, `security_detection` **DET-**.
Relationships: security incident ↔ IT incident/problem/CI/user/observable; vulnerable item → CI → service (criticality) → remediation group → change; risk ↔ control (mitigates), service/CI/supplier (affects), incident (materialised), vulnerability, finding; control ↔ requirement (many-to-many) ↔ evidence.
Access scoping: SIR, observables, evidence and privacy records sit in a secure workspace (explicit membership, no admin bypass, read audit, excluded from offline replica); vulnerable items are visible to the CI support group (`group`) and owners (`service`) with classification `internal`; risks use risk owner (`own`), `unit_subtree` and `service`; audit workpapers limited to the audit team; auditor persona is read-only with SoD against admins; DLP and detections visible to security admins only.

### KPIs
- MTTD = mean(detected_at − first_malicious_at); MTTC = mean(contained_at − detected_at); MTTR = mean(recovered_at − detected_at), by category and severity; false-positive rate = incidents closed as benign ÷ incidents.
- Vulnerability SLA % = items fixed within target ÷ items fixed, per severity; mean time to remediate = mean(fixed_at − first_detected_at); open-item age buckets; KEV overdue = KEV items past due ÷ KEV items.
- Scan coverage = in-scope assets scanned in 7/30 days ÷ in-scope assets; patch compliance = compliant assets ÷ assets; open and expired exceptions.
- Control pass rate = passed tests ÷ tests; % controls tested in last 6 months; compliance % per framework = implemented-and-effective requirements ÷ applicable; CSF gap = Σ(target − current tier).
- Open and overdue audit findings; finding age; policy attestation rate = acknowledged ÷ targeted.
- Risks above appetite; % risks with owner, likelihood, impact, treatment and next-action date; % risks reviewed in 6 months; % treatment actions on time; residual-risk trend; risk-culture ratio = risks reported by non-risk roles ÷ risks.
- Regulatory timeliness = notifications filed before deadline ÷ required; % staff trained; phishing-simulation click rate; dormant privileged accounts; open SoD conflicts.

### Beyond the market
- Iranian rule packs authored as data (AFTA security requirements, passive-defence guidance, sector regulators such as the central bank) with Persian templates for national CERT reporting; exact obligations verified per customer, not hard-coded.
- Air-gapped operation: CVE/EPSS/KEV feeds imported as signed offline bundles; open-source scanners (Greenbone, Wazuh, Trivy) first-class where commercial scanners are unavailable.
- Persian PII detectors with validation (national ID checksum, IBAN mod-97, Luhn cards, 09xx mobiles, 10-digit postal codes) and Persian phishing classification (fake judicial e-notices, bank SMS lures).
- Security summaries, observable analysis and regulatory mapping on the tenant's local model; no vendor telemetry.
- Signed approvals and alerts through Bale/Eitaa bots and SMS; secure workspaces never sync to the offline replica, and lost devices are wiped remotely.
- GRC native to ITSM data: continuous control monitoring reads changes, access requests and vulnerabilities directly, which bolt-on GRC tools cannot.

## Deliberately excluded
- **ESG management** (SN IRM ESG) — corporate sustainability reporting, not ITSM; sustainability attributes live in the platform.
- **Vendor compliance programmes** (HIPAA BAA, FedRAMP/government cloud, vendor SOC 2 certificates) — attestations of a SaaS vendor; our self-hosted tenants get the controls and packs (SEC-030, SEC-050) instead.
- **SSO, SCIM, MFA, IP allowlists, mobile MAM/MDM policies** (Atlassian Guard Standard) — owned by the access/admin modules.
- **Shadow-IT discovery** — owned by ITAM SaaS management.
- **Day-pass licence metering** (FS occasional agents) — a pricing mechanism; the occasional-agent persona belongs to access.
- **Full contact-centre WFM** (intraday re-forecasting, shift bidding, payroll export, ACD/IVR adherence) — dedicated WFM products; we keep forecasting, staffing and basic adherence (WFM-019–021).
- **On-call paging and escalation policies; field crew optimisation; project resource management** — owned by event, field_service and project; workforce only shares skills, shifts and availability (WFM-024, WFM-044).
- **Cross-customer peer benchmarks** (SN Benchmarks) — needs pooling data across tenants, which conflicts with data sovereignty; internal benchmarking only (ANA-035).
- **Vendor-specific analytics migrations and stores** (SN Platform Analytics Migration Center, MetricBase, RaptorDB, Atlassian Data Lake family schemas) — not applicable; we define our own analytics store.
- **Usage-based analytics pricing limits** (FS fair-usage) — commercial; we keep technical query guardrails only.
- **Emotion recognition of agents from voice or face** — prohibited (EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f)) and unethical; text sentiment on tickets only.
- **Individual leaderboards tied to pay** — excluded by design; gamification is opt-in and team-oriented.
- **SN Automation Discovery** — deprecated by its vendor; replaced by ANA-032.

## Engine requirements
1. **Business-time service** — calendars with IANA zones, slots, holidays (Jalali, Gregorian and lunar-holiday computation), special hours, hierarchy; add/subtract/diff in business time; working-minute index; recompute hooks. Shared by SLA, OLA, workforce, KPIs, on-call.
2. **Event-sourced timer engine** — per-(record, definition) state machines (attach/start/pause/resume/stop/cancel/reset), multi-cycle, deterministic replay from record history, dry-run simulation, timer wheel/scheduled jobs for milestones re-planned on change.
3. **Condition AST evaluator & ordered policy resolver** — one AST for applicability, clock, routing, DLP and rule conditions; first-match ordering; hierarchical overrides (domain → company → unit/site → workspace); versioned, effective-dated config with snapshot-at-use.
4. **Planned-interval scheduler** — RRULE (Jalali-aware) for reviews, collections, deliveries, control tests, attestations, renewals; idempotent per (rule, period) with evidence capture.
5. **Survey & assessment engine** — questionnaires, weighted scoring, sampling, anonymity thresholds; reused by CSAT/CES/NPS/Happiness, QA evaluations, risk and maturity assessments, control self-assessments and attestations.
6. **Semantic metric layer & query compiler** — metrics, dimensions and glossary compiled to SQL with row-level predicates, field masks, k-anonymity, query ACLs and guardrails; same compiler for lists, reports, dashboards, exports, NL questions and AI tools.
7. **Analytics store** — CDC into star-schema facts (incl. `fact_state_interval`, `fact_sla_instance`, `fact_ai_artifact`), snapshot collector with drill-back ids, watermark recompute, freshness indicator; works embedded on-prem.
8. **Statistics & ML library** — STL/Holt-Winters forecasting, change-point, SPC rules, Mann–Kendall, contribution analysis, survival models with calibration, Erlang C/A, clustering and embeddings; jobs run within worker budgets.
9. **AI skill framework** — run-as-user, grounded generation from computed tables, function calling over the semantic layer, explanation records, proposal-only writes with approvals, model routing per skill (cloud or local), `ai_run` logging with cost.
10. **Routing engine** — queues, eligibility (team, skill, shift, presence, capacity), strategies, offers with timeouts, decision log; presence tracking and RRULE shift materialisation.
11. **Restriction & audit services** — secure workspaces, classification restrictions, read audit, hash-chained privileged log, DLP policy checks in every exporter, sharer, API and AI retriever; offline-replica exclusions.
12. **Risk & control engine** — versioned scales, likelihood × impact matrices, appetite thresholds, control tests and indicators as scheduled metric queries, many-to-many requirement ↔ control ↔ evidence.
13. **Regulatory clock engine** — calendar-time deadlines per regime with templates and submission evidence.
14. **Connector & ingestion framework** — scanners, SIEM/EDR, TI feeds, calendar sync, BI; normalisation, deduplication, CI identification, signed offline bundles.
15. **Notification channels** — e-mail, SMS gateways, Bale/Eitaa/Telegram bots, Teams/Slack, push; delivery logs as evidence; permission re-check at send time.
16. **Document generation** — Persian/RTL PDF, XLSX and PPTX with Jalali dates and correct shaping for agreements, review packs, reports and evidence packs.
