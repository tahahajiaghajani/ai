# Service requests and catalog, self-service portal and virtual agent, enterprise service management, customer service, field service

Consolidated, deduplicated feature catalog for module group **g4** (keys `request`, `portal`, `esm`, `csm`, `field_service`). It merges the research reports `itil.md`, `standards.md`, `servicenow.md`, `jsm.md`, `freshservice.md`, `bmc_manageengine.md`, `others.md`, `ai.md`, `access.md` and `analytics_ux.md` (snapshot 2026-11-07). Each capability appears once, in its most complete variant, with the tools and frameworks that have it.

**Scope and boundaries**
- `request`: service request management and the request catalogue (ITIL 4 SRM; ISO/IEC 20000-1 §8.6.2; FitSM PR9; COBIT DSS02; IT4IT Request-to-Fulfil).
- `portal`: self-service portal, intake channels, interactions, virtual agent front door, walk-up, surveys and requester communications (ITIL 4 service desk; WCAG 2.2; EU AI Act Art. 50).
- `esm`: department workspaces (HR, facilities, finance, legal, procurement, compliance), employee journeys, reservations, visitors, speak-up (ISO/IEC 20000-1 for any service; USM; VeriSM; ISO 41001; ISO 45001; ISO 37002).
- `csm`: external customers — accounts, contacts, entitlements, install base, cases, escalations, complaints, relationship management (ITIL 4 relationship management and DSV; ISO 10002; eTOM).
- `field_service`: work orders, dispatch, scheduling, mobile and offline technicians, parts, planned maintenance.
- Consumed from other groups, not repeated: the record, form, flow, approval, SLA, notification and survey engines (`platform`); audiences, scopes and field classes (`access`); assistant, router, RAG, tools and governance (`ai`, referenced as g5 `AI-nnn`); knowledge bases and communities (`knowledge`); offerings and subscriptions (`service_portfolio`); stock, models, loaners and procurement (`itam`); contracts (`supplier`); skills, shifts and contact-centre WFM (`workforce`); SLA definitions (`slm`).

**Conventions**

## Module: request — مدیریت درخواست خدمت و کاتالوگ خدمات

Handles pre-defined, user-initiated service requests "in an effective and user-friendly manner" (ITIL 4): a catalogue of request models, ordering through every channel, approvals, fulfilment (manual, automated or agentic) and feedback. Requests are a normal part of service delivery, standardised and automated as far as possible; ISO/IEC 20000-1 §8.6.2 requires them to be recorded, classified, prioritised, fulfilled and closed.

### Features

**Catalogue definition**

- **REQ-001 Request catalogues and categories** · کاتالوگ‌های درخواست و دسته‌بندی — Several catalogues (IT, HR, facilities, vendor) per workspace, each with a nested category tree (icon, description, manual or A–Z order), agent-side request-type groups for queues and reports, and per-portal curation. *Src:* SN, JSM, FS, BMC DWP, SDP, GLPI, ITIL 4 · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `catalog`, `catalog_category` (tree) on E01; audience bindings E11.
- **REQ-002 Catalogue item (request model)** · آیتم کاتالوگ (مدل درخواست) — Name, short and rich description, media, offering and related articles, owner, quantity/cart/Requested-for switches, button label, channel availability, multilingual help text, and links to form, approval policy, fulfilment flow and SLA. *Src:* SN, FS, BMC, SDP, JSM, ITIL 4, FitSM PR9 · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `catalog_item` (prefix ITM) referencing E04, E05, E07, E08.
- **REQ-003 Item types** · انواع آیتم — Standard item, record producer, order guide/bundle, content item (link to article or URL), standard-change item, access item, software item, hardware/model item and loaner item, each with its own fulfilment defaults. *Src:* SN, FS, BMC, SDP, JSM · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `catalog_item.type` choice driving form sections and default flows.
- **REQ-004 Record producers** · فرم‌های ایجاد رکورد — A catalogue form that creates a record on any table (incident, HR case, facilities request, customer case, change) instead of a requested item, mapping answers to fields with a post-processing rule; the basis of "Report an issue" and most ESM intake. *Src:* SN, BMC, SDP, JSM, GLPI 11 · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `catalog_item.target_table` + `field_map`; create through E01 with the requester's grants.
- **REQ-005 Dynamic form variables** · متغیرهای فرم پویا — Question types: text, number, money, email, URL, IP, phone, Jalali date/date-time, duration, select, radio, multi-select, yes/no, rating, reference, list collector, Requested-for, location, CI/asset picker, attachment, label/section and masked secret; mandatory, read-only, hidden, defaults, reference qualifiers and per-question roles; answers stored typed for rules, SLAs and reports. *Src:* SN, FS, BMC, SDP, JSM, GLPI · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* E04 renderer shared by every channel; typed `request_answer` facts.
- **REQ-006 Variable sets and shared question library** · مجموعه‌پرسش‌ها و کتابخانهٔ مشترک — Reusable question sets and workspace-wide shared fields (archive/unarchive) used across items, so a change to "cost centre" or "delivery address" applies everywhere. *Src:* SN, FS (shared fields), BMC · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `variable_set`, `catalog_variable.set_id`; references resolved at publish time.
- **REQ-007 Multi-row question grids** · جدول پرسش چندسطری — Grid questions (rows of typed columns, max rows, collapsible) such as "list of users to onboard" or "items to move"; answers as JSON arrays with a schema. *Src:* SN (MRVS), BMC (repeatable groups) · **P1** · AI: no · *Impl:* `variable_set.multi_row`; JSON Schema validation server-side.
- **REQ-008 Form rules and validation in every channel** · قواعد و اعتبارسنجی فرم در همهٔ کانال‌ها — Declarative condition→action rules (show, hide, mandatory, read-only, set or clear value, filter choices) per item, set or task view, plus regex, range and cross-field checks, run in the browser and again on the server for portal, mobile, chat, AI and API. *Src:* SN catalog UI policies, FS business rules, JSM conditional forms, SDP, BMC · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* E03 AST compiled to client and server evaluators; no client-only scripts.
- **REQ-009 Data look-ups and dynamic defaults** · جست‌وجوی داده و پیش‌فرض پویا — Answers auto-filled from matcher tables or the Requested-for person (manager, unit, location, assets), dependent lookup fields over custom objects and locations, and REST data connections for choices. *Src:* SN, FS lookup fields, JSM data connections, BMC · **P1** · AI: no · *Impl:* E04 lookup providers; outbound calls via E26 with caching.
- **REQ-010 Item versioning and order-time snapshot** · نسخه‌بندی آیتم و ثبت نسخه هنگام سفارش — Items have draft, review, published and retired versions; every requested item stores the definition version and answers, so later edits never corrupt history or running flows. *Src:* SN, FS, ITIL 4 notes · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `catalog_item_version` (schema JSON); `request_item.item_version_id`.
- **REQ-011 No-code catalogue builder with guardrails** · سازندهٔ بی‌کد کاتالوگ با محافظ — Guided steps (details, questions, rules, access, fulfilment, review) for editors; admin templates lock values and limit question types, flows and approvals; preview as a persona; publish as a versioned change set; several editors per item. *Src:* SN Catalog Builder, BMC, FS, SDP · **P1** · AI: no · *Impl:* builder on E16; `catalog_template` guardrails.
- **REQ-012 Catalogue roles and item ownership** · نقش‌های کاتالوگ و مالکیت آیتم — Catalogue admin, manager and editor roles scoped to catalogues or workspaces; item owner and secondary owners maintain content and receive feedback. *Src:* SN, BMC, FS, SDP · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* role-in-context grants on `catalog` (E11).
- **REQ-013 Audiences and entitlement checks** · مخاطبان و کنترل استحقاق — "Available for / not available for" on catalogues, categories and items (users, groups, units ± subtree, locations, companies, roles, conditions; deny wins) plus offering, licence and stock checks, evaluated at listing and again at submit in every channel; unidentified channels get public items only. *Src:* SN user criteria, JSM restricted types, FS, SDP, COBIT DSS02.03 · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `audience_binding(can_request)` keys (E11); re-check in the create API.
- **REQ-014 Hidden, restricted and featured items** · آیتم‌های پنهان، محدود و برجسته — Hidden items (not listed but searchable or linkable), restricted items (named users/groups/organisations only), pinned/featured items and catalogue banners. *Src:* JSM, GLPI 12, BMC DWP · **P1** · AI: no · *Impl:* flags on `catalog_item`; search index honours them.
- **REQ-015 Prices and recurring charges** · قیمت و هزینهٔ دوره‌ای — One-off and recurring price (frequency), price per answer (e.g., +RAM), totals in the cart, Rial/Toman display, cost visibility switch for requesters, cost centre on the order. *Src:* SN, FS, BMC, SDP, IT4IT · **P1** · AI: no · *Impl:* money fields (`financial` class); price rules in E03.
- **REQ-016 Delivery-time promise** · زمان تحویل وعده‌داده‌شده — Estimated delivery per item in business days, shown at order and turned into a due date on the requested item using the business calendar (Iranian holidays). *Src:* SN, FS, ITIL 4 expectation management · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* E06 working-day arithmetic; `request_item.due_at`.
- **REQ-017 Service level expectations (SLE)** · انتظار سطح خدمت آماری — Probabilistic promise derived from history ("85% of laptops within 5 working days"), shown beside the contractual target and recomputed per item and class of service over a rolling window (p50/p85/p95). *Src:* Kanban flow metrics · **P1** · AI: no · *Impl:* nightly E13 job over cycle times; never averaged across classes.

**Ordering**

- **REQ-018 Cart and checkout** · سبد و تکمیل سفارش — Cart with quantities, order now, save for later/wish list, special instructions, delivery location and date, attachments on the cart, two-step checkout with summary and totals; one order creates one request with one requested item per line. *Src:* SN, BMC DWP, FS, IT4IT (shopping cart) · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `cart`, `cart_line`; checkout in one transaction (E01).
- **REQ-019 Request on behalf of others** · ثبت درخواست برای دیگران — Requested-by and Requested-for are separate; scopes for ordering for others (anyone in company, my reportees, my unit subtree, my delegators); agents raise on behalf from a create dialog; approvals and entitlement use the Requested-for, notifications go to both. *Src:* SN, FS, JSM, BMC · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* permission `request.on_behalf` with scope (E11); both users get `req:` keys.
- **REQ-020 Multi-user requests** · درخواست گروهی برای چند نفر — Order one item for many people at once (list collector or CSV), creating one requested item per person with individual approvals and tracking. *Src:* BMC DWP, SN, FS (bulk onboarding) · **P1** · AI: no · *Impl:* checkout fan-out; idempotency key per person.
- **REQ-021 Order guides and bundles** · راهنمای سفارش و بسته‌ها — Scenario guides (new hire, office move) ask initial questions, a rule base includes items and answers cascade to them; bundles add mandatory or optional child items that spawn child requests (own group and SLA) or stay one request. *Src:* SN order guides, FS bundles, BMC, Snipe-IT kits · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `order_guide_rule` (AST → item, cascade map); expansion at checkout.
- **REQ-022 Three-level request structure** · ساختار سه‌سطحی درخواست — Request (order header) → requested item (per line: answers, approval, stage, SLA, priority from impact × urgency) → fulfilment task (per group, provider or automation); the request closes when all items close, partially fulfilled when mixed. *Src:* SN, FS, ITIL 4, IT4IT, BMC SRM · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `request` (REQ), `request_item` (RITM), `request_task` (RTSK) on the task base; roll-up hook.
- **REQ-023 Stages, progress and status updates** · مرحله‌ها، پیگیری پیشرفت و اطلاع‌رسانی — A requester-facing stage (approval → sourcing → fulfilment → delivery → completed, customisable per item), separate from workflow state, shown as a progress bar with due-date warnings and proactive notifications. *Src:* SN, FS, BMC, JSM, ITIL 4 · **P0** · AI: no · *Impl:* `request_item.stage` per item version; E09 templates.

Relationships: request 1–N request_item 1–N request_task; request_item → catalog_item_version, offering, cost centre, linked change/incident (`record_link`), asset or `access_grant` produced; approvals (`approval`, E05) and SLA instances (E08) attach to RITM and RTSK.

Access scoping: `own` = requested_by_id, requested_for_id, opened_by_id; `participant` = watch_list, approvers (time-boxed share), shared organisation; `group` = assignment_group_id (tasks add their group keys to the parent); `unit`/`reportees`/`location` snapshotted from the Requested-for person; `company_id`, `domain_id`, `workspace_id` on every row; requesters see only requester-visible tasks and public fields; answers obey field classes.

### KPIs

- Fulfilment time per item = closed_at − submitted_at (p50/p85/p95, not only mean); time in stage per stage.
- Within commitment % = items fulfilled ≤ due/promised date ÷ items fulfilled; SLA attainment on RITM and RTSK.
- Approval cycle time = avg(approved_at − approval_requested_at); rejection rate = rejected ÷ requested.
- First-time-right = items not returned to the requester for information ÷ items.
- Zero-touch rate = items fulfilled fully by automation or agents ÷ items; % catalogue items with automated fulfilment.
- Self-service ordering rate = items via portal, chat or assistant ÷ all items.
- Cart/form abandonment = (started − submitted) ÷ started.
- Catalogue completeness = 1 − ad hoc items ÷ items; deviation rate = tasks flagged deviation ÷ tasks.
- Waiting-on-requester share = time in waiting-for-requester ÷ total time.
- Reopen/rework rate; backlog and work-item age; cost per request = (labour + parts + licences) ÷ requests; CSAT per item; incidents caused by incorrect fulfilment.

### Beyond the market

- Delivery promises and SLEs computed on the Jalali calendar with Iranian solar and lunar holidays and Thursday half-days, explained in plain Persian ("حدود ۵ روز کاری").
- One-tap approvals in Bale, Eitaa, Rubika or Telegram and by SMS short links, with optional Iranian PKI digital signatures for financial approvals.
- Ordering from the offline Windows replica: carts and approvals queue locally and are re-validated on reconnect.
- Every catalogue item is automatically an AI tool on the tenant's own (even local) model, so conversational ordering works without sending data abroad.
- Rial/Toman prices with optional periodic price indexation and procurement hand-off to Iranian ERPs (e.g., Rahkaran, Hamkaran Sistem, Sepidar) through connectors.
- Honest SLE and abandonment analytics that most vendors lack; catalogue gaps mined from Persian conversations.

## Module: portal — درگاه سلف‌سرویس، دستیار مجازی و کانال‌های ارتباطی

### KPIs

- Self-service share = contacts via portal, assistant and messengers ÷ all contacts; channel mix per channel.
- Deflection rate = self-service sessions with no ticket on the same topic within 72 h ÷ sessions; containment = assistant conversations ended without hand-off ÷ conversations.
- First contact resolution = resolved during first contact ÷ contacts; first-time-right routing = records not reassigned after triage ÷ records.
- Average speed of answer, abandonment rate = abandoned ÷ offered (chat, phone), average handle time, time to triage.
- Search success = searches followed by a click and no ticket ÷ searches; zero-result rate.
- Walk-up average wait and service time; appointment no-show rate.
- CSAT = satisfied responses ÷ responses; CES average; NPS = %promoters − %detractors; XLA happiness and lost time.
- Portal adoption = unique monthly users ÷ employees; contacts per user per month; cost per contact by channel; approval turnaround.

- First-day readiness = new hires with every day-1 item delivered ÷ new hires.
- Journey cycle time = completed_at − started_at; on-time activity rate = activities done by due date ÷ activities.
- Cross-department hand-off delay = Σ waiting time between department tasks per journey or request.
- Self-service share and CSAT per department; case reopen rate; time to resolve per COE and HR service.
- Confidential-case access violations (target 0); break-glass uses reviewed within 24 h.
- Reservation utilisation = checked-in booked hours ÷ available hours; no-show rate = no-shows ÷ bookings.
- Visitor wait time at reception; speak-up acknowledgement within 7 days % and feedback within 3 months %.
- Safety: incident and near-miss rates per 200,000 hours worked; corrective actions closed on time %.

### Data model

| Table (prefix) | Key fields | States → category |
|---|---|---|
| `work_order` (WO) | parent ref, company/account, contact, location/site, access notes, asset/ci, symptoms, priority, coverage, billing type, on-site SLA | draft, qualification→new; scheduled, in_progress→active; awaiting_parts, awaiting_customer→waiting; work_complete→resolved; closed_complete, closed_incomplete→closed; cancelled→canceled |
| `wo_task` (WOT) | work_order_id, template, skills (min level), certifications, parts, est work/travel, window, appointment, technician or crew, actual travel/arrival/start/end, break segments, revisit reason, signature | pending_dispatch→new; assigned, accepted, en_route, on_site→active; on_hold, pending_parts→waiting; work_complete→resolved; closed→closed; cancelled→canceled |
| `wo_task_template`, `territory`, `crew`, `schedule_run` | task model; zone, calendar, dispatch group; members, lead; horizon, objectives, results, dropped tasks with reasons | — |
| `parts_requirement` | task, model, quantity, source stockroom | reserved→new, picked→active, consumed→closed, returned/cancelled→canceled |
| `maintenance_plan` (PMP), `meter_reading` | asset or class, trigger (time/meter/condition), RRULE, template, lead time, next due | active→active, paused→waiting, retired→closed |
| `time_entry`, `expense_entry`, `field_location_ping`, `ops_note` | task, type, duration or amount; user, task, timestamp, coordinates (short retention) | — |

Access scoping: `assigned` (technician) and crew members; `group` = dispatch group; `location_subtree`/territory for dispatchers and managers; `company_id` subtree so customers see their own work orders in the portal; contractors get `assigned` + vendor-group scope and only fields marked for vendors; the offline set is the technician's scope only (FSM-018); location pings visible to dispatch roles only.

### KPIs

- First-time fix rate = tasks completed on the first visit ÷ completed tasks.
- On-site response attainment = arrivals within target ÷ arrivals; schedule adherence = arrivals within the booked window ÷ visits.
- Mean time to dispatch = avg(dispatched_at − created_at).
- Travel ratio = travel time ÷ (travel + work time); utilisation = work time ÷ available time; jobs per technician per day.
- Repeat-visit rate within N days; parts availability = tasks not delayed by parts ÷ tasks; no-access rate.
- Planned-maintenance compliance = PM work orders done by due date ÷ due; contractor SLA compliance; post-visit CSAT.

### Beyond the market

- Routing and travel times from Iranian map services (e.g., Neshan, Balad) with local traffic patterns, Friday weekend, Thursday half-days and lunar holidays in the calendar.
- Offline-first technician work for low-connectivity regions on Windows tablets and phones, with encrypted, permission-exact data and Persian voice notes transcribed on the device or server.
- ETA and visit confirmations by SMS and local messengers instead of foreign apps.
- An on-device or on-premises model for guided troubleshooting when the site has no internet.
- Earthquake and disaster dispatch mode: mass re-prioritisation of site inspections with safety check-ins.
## Deliberately excluded

- **Sales CRM depth** (opportunity pipelines, quoting/CPQ, product ordering such as TMF622) — outside service management; only customer demand intake is kept (CSM-036).
- **Full IWMS** (lease administration, CAD space planning, energy management) — we keep locations, spaces, bookings, visitors and maintenance (ESM-026…033).
- **Legal spend and outside-counsel e-billing, matter budgeting** — specialist legal software territory; intake, matters and holds are kept.
- **Full HCM, payroll, recruiting (ATS) and LMS** — integrated, not rebuilt (ESM-024, ESM-025); journeys orchestrate them.
- **Contact-centre workforce optimisation** (forecasting, scheduling, quality) — owned by the `workforce` module.
- **Channels with no Iranian user base**: Facebook Messenger, LINE, Apple Messages for Business, Workplace from Facebook (discontinued), Yammer; public social-media intake (X, Facebook pages). A generic webhook channel adapter covers future needs; WhatsApp stays P2.
- **Phone-number purchase and porting inside the product** — tenants connect their own SIP trunks and PBX (Asterisk/Issabel).
- **Vendor commercial constructs** (portal-visit caps per fulfiller, per-resolution price tiers, assisted-conversation packs) — only tenant-side AI budgets and quotas are kept (g5 AI-168).
- **Proprietary scripting models** (AngularJS widgets, GlideAjax client scripts, legacy workflows and execution plans) — replaced by declarative form rules (REQ-008), flows and a widget registry.
- **Third-party web analytics in the portal** (e.g., Google Analytics) — privacy and sovereignty; built-in portal analytics instead (PTL-050).
- **Government-cloud variants** (FedRAMP, IL5, IRAP) — not applicable; sovereign on-premises and local-model deployment covers the need.
- **Workplace emotion recognition** of employees — prohibited by EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f) and inappropriate; only text sentiment on customer and service records (CSM-041).
- **Kiosk and badge-printer hardware drivers** — integrated through generic print and device APIs.
