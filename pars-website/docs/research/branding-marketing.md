# Branding & marketing research — B2B enterprise / AI (2025–2026)

> "(vendor)" marks numbers published by vendors. Collected 2026-10-22.

## 1. Frameworks applied to Pars
- **Brand platform:** the logo already encodes the idea — *convergence*: many flows (projects, data, agents, people) meeting at one decision point.
- **Archetypes:** Sage (primary: 15 years + academy) × Magician (secondary: agents) ([Umbrex](https://umbrex.com/resources/frameworks/marketing-frameworks/brand-archetypes-framework-jungian-archetypes/), [Britopian](https://www.britopian.com/content/b2b-brand-archetypes/)).
- **Category design (Play Bigger):** name and own a category, e.g. "Agentic PMO" (دفتر پروژه‌ی عامل‌محور); launch with one "lightning strike" ([summary](https://www.antoinebuteau.com/play-bigger-book-summary/)); cf. Salesforce "digital labor" ([Tech Brew](https://www.techbrew.com/stories/2025/01/13/salesforce-digital-labor-ai-tools)).
- **Positioning (April Dunford):** alternatives → unique attributes → value/proof → target → category. In Iranian banks the real alternative is Excel + manual PMO reporting ([MTPCon 2026](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/everything-has-changed-except-for-the-fundamentals-of-positioning-april-dunford-at-mtpcon-london-2026)).
- **StoryBrand:** the bank's PMO head is the hero; Pars is the guide (empathy + authority), 3-step plan, one CTA ([guide](https://www.gravityglobal.com/blog/complete-guide-storybrand-framework)).
- **JTBD:** switch interviews, four forces (push, pull, anxiety, habit) ([BoS](https://businessofsoftware.org/talks/understanding-your-customer-jtbd/)).
- **Motion identity:** motion is now core brand, AI/fintech lead it ([Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/insight/motion-brandings-moment-why-brands-need-to-catch-up/)). The ribbon becomes a motion system: converge = done, flow = agent working, split = risk.
- **95:5 rule:** ~5% of B2B buyers are in-market; build memory with the other 95%, keep distinctive assets consistent ([Dreamdata](https://dreamdata.io/blog/the-95-5-rule-john-dawes)).

## 2. 2025–2026 B2B methods
- 61% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience ([Gartner](https://gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-sales-survey-finds-61-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-buying-experience)); 94% use AI while buying ([Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/b2b_buyers_make_zero_click_buying_number_one/)).
- "AI washing" signals risk; vendors that show what systems do, where they fail and how they're governed keep growing ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/7622129)) → the "90% automation" claim needs a published method.
- **GEO:** statistics, citations and quotations raise visibility in AI answers by up to ~40% (Princeton GEO paper, arXiv 2311.09735); off-site brand mentions are the strongest predictor for Google AI Overviews ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)); llms.txt shows no measurable benefit — cheap to add only ([SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/)).
- Founder-led LinkedIn: Thought Leader Ads ≈1.7× CTR of single-image ads ([ZenABM](https://zenabm.com/blog/linkedin-ads-ctr-benchmarks), vendor).
- Interactive demos ≈3× demo-to-opportunity conversion ([Navattic](https://navattic.com/report/state-of-the-interactive-product-demo-2025), vendor). AI chat: ~17% of chat sessions book a demo (weak source).
- Dark social: add a free-text "How did you hear about us?" field ([Refine Labs](https://www.refinelabs.com/blog/attribution-mirage)).
- ABM fits Iran's small number of banks; case studies are the #1 format for 57% of marketing leaders ([Demand Gen Report](https://www.demandgenreport.com/blog/the-2025-demand-generation-benchmark-survey-scaling-personalization-optimizing-operations-tying-strategy-to-revenue-in-2025/49804/)). A trust center shortens security reviews.

## 3. Iran specifics
- 2026: whitelist internet after the February strikes, partial restoration in May ([AP](https://ktla.com/news/nationworld/ap-international/ap-iranians-are-back-online-after-a-monthslong-shutdown-but-still-face-heavy-restrictions/amp/)) → keep a domestic mirror/hosting option, self-host fonts and scripts, no foreign CDNs.
- Channels: LinkedIn blocked but ~5.9M Iranian accounts (executives/diaspora); Instagram 56% / Telegram 39% of population (2024); Bale (bank-backed) for official comms — not E2E-encrypted, keep client data off it; Aparat for video.
- SEO: Google ≈99% share in Iran; AI Overviews support Persian; keep spelling and half-spaces (ZWNJ) consistent.
- Procurement: SETAD e-procurement for tender-law entities; informatics rating (grades 1–7); AFTA-licensed security; Central Bank rules for banking software.
- Events: Elecomp; Iran finance industry exhibition (banking, insurance, exchange); Central Bank's Modern Banking & Payment Systems conference; International Project Management Conference.
- PMI reportedly restricts certification for Iranian addresses → position the academy on practical skills, not only exams.

## 4. Ten priority actions (first 6 months)
1. Positioning sprint (8–10 switch interviews with bank PMO heads) — KPI: ≥80% pass a 5-second test.
2. Website as a proof engine — Persian-first, domestic availability, one page per customer job, comparison pages — KPI: LCP < 2.5 s, demo-request conversion ≥ 2%.
3. Anti-AI-washing proof library — 6 quantified case studies showing what agents do and where humans approve.
4. Interactive demos + the Persian RAG assistant on the site — KPI: demo completion ≥ 30%, chat→meeting rate.
5. Trust center — data residency, on-prem/local LLM, AFTA, informatics rating, audit logs, human-in-the-loop.
6. ABM for ~30 banks/financial institutions with a tailored PMO maturity report.
7. CEO + 2 experts publish weekly (LinkedIn, mirrored to Telegram/Bale); "State of AI in Bank PMOs" report.
8. Pars Academy funnel — free webinars, PSM/PMP/EPM with product labs, alumni community.
9. GEO + Persian SEO — identical company description everywhere, Organization/Product/FAQ schema, statistics-rich answer pages.
10. Lightning-strike launch at a flagship banking/PM event with a live agent demo; video series on Aparat.
